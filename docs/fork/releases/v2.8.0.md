**Noctis Plus 2.8.0**: based on Noctis 1.6.9, with search by lyrics, Other Versions and word timing choices.

## Added

- **Search by lyrics.** The Songs search box can look in lyrics too, or only in lyrics; the matching line shows under the song.
- **Other Versions.** A song's menu (and the player's ⋯ menu) lists its other recordings in your library (live, remastered, instrumental…) and switches to one.
- **Word timing choices.** Clicking the lyrics credit (for example "Word timing from Kugou") opens Search Lyrics: the online timing, WordLyrics' timing, Time with WordLyrics and Find online word timing sit above the lyrics sources.
- **Better lyrics, by themselves.** Settings › Lyrics can replace a song's plain lyrics with synced ones when they are found; lyrics can also be found for a whole album or for new songs.
- **Your sidebar.** Settings › General: hide pages, reorder them and pick the page Noctis opens on.
- **Multichannel output.** 5.1 and 7.1 files play on every speaker, with mono, balance and the visualizers working on them.
- From Noctis 1.6.8 and 1.6.9 as well: a waveform progress bar and a background behind the sidebar on the lyrics page, Quick Playlist and Trash buttons for the player bar, a lyrics page that centres the player for a song with no lyrics (it says under the player what is happening), queued scrobbles when Last.fm or ListenBrainz cannot be reached, and artists shown as written in the tags.

## Fixed

- At the end of a For you page the next page plays, also after Not for me on the page's last song.
- Everything Noctis 1.6.8 and 1.6.9 fixed (queue, Repeat All after Clear Queue, MP3 lengths, menus and more).

## Install or update

Existing installations can update through Noctis Plus. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.8.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
