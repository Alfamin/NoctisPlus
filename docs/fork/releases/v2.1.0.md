**Noctis Plus 2.1.0.** Find playlists, and hear the song you clicked right away.

## What's new

- **Playlists in Search.** Type a name ("gym", "hype drake") to find public Spotify playlists, ranked by how many people saved them, with Deezer as backup. Open one to see what you have and what's missing, play it, then **Save** it as your own editable playlist. More load as you scroll.
- **Your last click plays first.** The song you press Play on starts at once; earlier downloads wait until it's done, and the song you're listening to keeps going.
- **Faster bots.** The link reaches the bot in about a second, and a file starts downloading the moment the bot sends it.
  - **First bot:** choose Music Hunters or DeezLoad (Settings › Free Music Finder); the other is the reserve.
  - **Daily search limit:** when Music Hunters hits it, the song's link is sent instead.
  - **Busy bot:** "please wait for the current download" no longer stalls; the other bot is asked and whichever answers first is used.
  - **Silent DeezLoad:** it is skipped after a few seconds instead of 20.
- **Fewer missed songs.** Apple-catalog songs get a link for the bots; small length differences between catalogs (SZA "Saturn") and censored titles now match.
- **Smoother lyrics.** Finishing word timing no longer flashes or freezes the lyrics on screen.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.1.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
