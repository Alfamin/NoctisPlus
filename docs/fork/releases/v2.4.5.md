**Noctis Plus 2.4.5**: The music bots are ready the moment you sign in.

## What's new

- **Music bots ready on sign-in.** Both download bots now ask new accounts to join their channel before they send songs. Right after you connect Telegram, Noctis Plus checks and joins both channels for you, in about a second. There is nothing to press.

## Fixed

- A bot that asks for its channel later, during a download, is joined by itself; the song carries on and the other bot fills in meanwhile.
- The QR code page says plainly to scan with the Telegram app, not the phone's camera, with the way there: Telegram › Settings › Devices › Add Device. The window carries Telegram's mark.

## Validation

Application suite: 6,834 tests, zero failures. Finder: 1,707 checks, zero failures, including both bots' real channel messages. Live on a real account after leaving both channels: the check found both in 1.1 seconds, joining took a few seconds, and both bots answered again. Owner check on a brand-new profile: QR sign-in, both channels joined automatically.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.4.5-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
