**Noctis Plus 2.5.0**: Word-by-word lyrics in seconds, without censored words.

## What's new

- **Word lyrics in seconds.** Before WordLyrics listens to a song, Noctis Plus asks lrc.red, Unison and Kugou for word timing at once and shows the first good answer, for songs on disk and streamed songs alike. Each answer is checked against the song itself; if Kugou's timing turns out not to fit, it stays on screen while WordLyrics quietly retimes it. WordLyrics only runs for songs no source has.
- **Censored words filled in.** lrc.red and Kugou often mask words ("****", "n**ga"). LRCLIB is asked at the same time, and each masked word takes the real word from its copy, found by the words around it; the timing is untouched. Lyrics already saved are fixed once in the background.
- **Seek while a song downloads.** Every seek bar, mini player and lyrics timeline included, shows how far a streaming song has arrived. Seeking past it waits there and plays as soon as that part is in.
- **Built on Noctis 1.6.5.** Its fixes, smart playlist counts, chapters and accessibility work are in; Plus keeps its own lyrics look and True Shuffle.

## Fixed

- Previous on a streamed song whose download was cut off plays it again instead of going forward.
- A streamed song played again keeps its online word timing, even half downloaded.
- Kugou lyrics no longer start with a block of credits.
- Retime on censored lyrics fetches fresh ones instead of saying it needs attention.
- Word timing runs in the background; its window lists the songs in line, with Remove.
- The result you press in Search downloads that very song when only its tags differ.
- Searching LRCLIB no longer fails when one of its entries has no length.

## Validation

Application suite: 7,947 tests, zero failures. Finder: 1,721 checks, zero failures. Censored words checked on real saved timings: 49 of 49 masked words filled correctly; live: Hot 13/13, a lot 16/16, Look Alive 14/14. Owner checks on the test build through the release day.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.5.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
