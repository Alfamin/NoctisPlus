**Noctis Plus 2.3.1** — a small update to For you and the queue.

## What's new

- **For you plays its page.** Playing a For you song queues the rest of the page you're looking at, from that song on. Songs download as the queue reaches them.
- **Download ahead.** Settings > Features > Free Music Finder: choose how many of the next songs you don't have download ahead, from Off to 4 (2 by default). Off downloads each song when it plays.

## Fixed

- **Not for me** on the song that's playing skips to the next one and takes it out of the queue.
- Every earlier For you page opens, including pages saved by 2.2.0, and the arrows move between them.

## Validation

Local validation: 6,783 application tests, zero failures; Finder: 1,580 checks, zero failures. Live checks on the owner's test profile: the page queue, Download ahead at Off and at 4 songs, and the setting's place in Settings. Not for me skipping the playing song has automated coverage only.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.3.1-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
