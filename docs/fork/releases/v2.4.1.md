**Noctis Plus 2.4.1**: bigger, better-mixed For you pages.

## What's new

- **Bigger For you pages.** Each page now has 25 songs, mixed by how well you know the artist: about half from your favourite artists (on every page, with different songs of theirs each time you refresh), and the rest from artists you play less, related artists you know, and artists new to you. Those take turns, so refreshing reaches more artists, while strong matches keep coming back. At most two songs by one artist per page.

## Fixed

- For you no longer suggests a song you already have under another name, such as a title with a censored word or another cut of the same song.
- The word timing pill no longer stays on "Starting WordLyrics" after a song that was being timed is skipped, and the backup copies word timing makes of streamed songs are removed once they are no longer needed.

## Validation

Local validation: 6,795 application tests, zero failures; Finder: 1,645 checks, zero failures. Live checks in a test profile: For you pages show 25 songs with the intended mix, refreshes bring new songs of the favourite artists, and earlier pages stay browsable. Replayed on a real listening history, the new pages find as many of the songs the listener went on to love as before, across more artists per page.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.4.1-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
