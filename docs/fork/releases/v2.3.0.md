**Noctis Plus 2.3.0** — For you that learns from your listening.

## What's new

- **For you learns as you listen.** Songs you play, keep, save or skip, and cards you scroll past without opening, shape the next page. Lasting favourites keep a place beside what you play this month. **Reset For you** in the shelf's menu clears what it learned.
- **Songs like your favourites.** Picks now also come from the songs you like most (Deezer's artist radio; no account or key needed), not only from their artists. With your own Last.fm key, Last.fm's similar songs join in; only artist names and the titles of up to 10 songs you like are sent.
- **Earlier pages kept.** Refresh keeps your last 10 pages. A small pager under the shelf (‹ 1 2 3 … 10 ›, 1 = newest) brings them back.
- **Fewer repeats.** A new page leaves the songs on your kept pages alone. Songs you've listened to or downloaded leave older pages when you go back to them.
- **Artists open straight away.** When several artists share a name, the best-known one loads immediately instead of asking which one you meant.
- **A quick skip isn't a no.** A song counts as heard after 30 seconds, a fifth of the song, or a real finish. Shorter tries can come back after 90 days; **Not for me** still hides a song for good.

## Fixed

- For you leaves out songs you've already heard, even ones you don't own, and shows one song once across remasters, clean editions and services.
- A catalog outage keeps your current picks instead of emptying the shelf; a slow service fails only its own artist.
- Building a page no longer runs on the app's UI thread, and the shelf no longer reacts to every layout change in the window.

## Validation and remaining checks

Local validation: 6,778 application tests, zero failures; Finder: 1,572 checks, zero failures. Live checks on the owner's test profile: picks, pages, pager, repeats and the artist page. The optional Last.fm similar-songs path has automated coverage only; no live run with a real key yet.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.3.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
