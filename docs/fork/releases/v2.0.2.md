**Noctis Plus 2.0.2.** Search the songs in front of you, instantly.

## What's new

- **Search inside Songs, playlists and Favorites.** Each page has its own search bar for titles, artists and albums. The navigation magnifier still opens unified search.
- **No extra typing delay.** Local search applies on the next UI dispatch and coalesces superseded edits. In a 2,134-song synthetic test, warm responses fell from about 170 ms to 15–16 ms; actual times vary by computer and library.
- **Keep your place.** Imported playlist songs remain searchable in source order, including songs you have not downloaded. Clear restores the list; pending edits cannot spill into another playlist.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.0.2-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
