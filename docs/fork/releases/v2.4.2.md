**Noctis Plus 2.4.2**: faster For you, working artist controls and clearer feedback.

## What's new

- **Don't recommend artist.** Available on For you cards, song menus, the player, artist pages and online Search. Songs with multiple credits let you choose one artist. Undo brings them back; Reset keeps your artist choices.
- **Not for me beside the lyrics.** Dismiss a song from For you without leaving the lyrics page.
- **Update or Later.** A startup prompt offers new versions when you're ready to update.

## Fixed

- The first For you refresh uses saved metadata immediately while slow catalog lookups refresh in the background.
- The Added check mark no longer carries over to the next streamed song.
- Artist actions work on their first menu opening and confirm the change. Reset For you visibly confirms and opens a folded shelf.

## Validation

Application suite: 6,805 total, zero failures, 43 skipped. Finder: 1,658 checks, zero failures. Real TEST-app clicks verified single- and multiple-artist menus, Undo, the song/player/artist/Search entry points, Reset, restart persistence and Recommend again. The startup update prompt is covered by deterministic fullscreen/non-fullscreen tests; an update from an older installed version was not exercised locally.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.4.2-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
