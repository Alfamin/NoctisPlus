**Noctis Plus 2.0.** Free Music Finder, WordLyrics, Lyric Motion and True Shuffle are now part of Noctis Plus itself instead of add-ons. Search is one page for your library and online music, artist, album and playlist pages show what you don't own yet, songs play while they download, and Telegram has a proper sign-in.

## Install

Paste this into PowerShell (no administrator needed):

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

It downloads `NoctisPlus-v2.0.0-Setup.exe` from this release through your system proxy/VPN, checks it against `SHA256SUMS`, installs it for your user and starts it. Or download the Setup below and run it yourself. The Setup is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: **More info > Run anyway**.

Already on 1.6.3.1, 1.6.3.2 or 1.6.3.3? Noctis Plus offers this update by itself. Noctis Plus now has its own version number; it is still based on Noctis 1.6.3, which is also the version plugins see, so plugins made for Noctis 1.6.3 keep working.

Noctis Plus installs next to official Noctis (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Noctis Plus`, data in `%APPDATA%\Noctis Plus`) and never changes, updates or removes it. The first start copies your official Noctis profile (library, play counts, playlists, sign-ins); official Noctis is only read.

## What's new

- **One Search page.** Your library and online music in one place, with Songs / Artists / Albums filters. The shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+F) and a song's "Find more by this artist…" open it. Back brings the same query, results and filter back.
- **Better ranking.** Popularity decides: "drake passionfruit" finds Drake's Passionfruit rather than covers, typing an artist's name lists songs by that artist, and a famous artist comes before a namesake (Runaway puts Kanye West first).
- **Play while it downloads.** Press Play on a song you don't have, in Search, on an artist, album or playlist page, or in For you, and it starts once enough has arrived; seeking unlocks once it is saved. Pressing Play again on the same song does not ask the bots again.
- **Artist, album and playlist pages show what you don't own.** Online albums open at once and fill in as they load. Imported Spotify and Deezer playlists list every song in its original order, owned or not, each with Play and Download; a downloaded song takes its place in the list. Browsing never downloads anything.
- **Preferred download quality.** Choose FLAC or MP3 in Features; FLAC can fall back to MP3 if you allow it. Each bot's setting is confirmed once and remembered. Songs whose length differs slightly between Apple Music and Deezer (for example Hold On, We're Going Home) are no longer refused.
- **WordLyrics is built in.** Word-by-word lyric timing, set up only when you choose to (it explains the download size first). A song without lyrics shows one **Time words** button, the lyrics page shows when a song is queued or being timed, and repairs live in the song's menu.
- **Native shuffle modes.** Equal chance, No repeats, Discovery, Never played or For you, from the player, mini player or Queue.
- **Lyric Motion is built into the lyrics**, including right-to-left lines, and the lyrics have a **Sync** button after you scroll away.
- **A new library starts with something to do:** Home offers Play, Add folder and Import until you have listening history.
- **Steadier playback:** a song that was paused for a long time comes back after a restart at the same spot, and a download-backed song that stops early continues from its saved file.
- **Downloads page**, **Spotify/Deezer playlist import**, and **Telegram sign-in** in Noctis's own style (Settings > Features > Free Music Finder > **Telegram account**).

Features live under **Settings > Features**, each with its own switch; your settings carry over. If you installed the separate Lyric Motion or Free Music Finder plugin, the built-in one is used instead, never both.

Known: opening Settings for the first time can pause for a moment. Downloads depend on the Telegram music bots; if one is busy or doesn't have a song, Noctis says so.

Don't run official Noctis and Noctis Plus at the same time: both use the same Telegram session, which can sign both out, and both would scrobble to Last.fm.

## Files

- `NoctisPlus-v2.0.0-Setup.exe`: the installer.
- `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip`: portable copy; extract to a new folder and run `Noctis.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums for both.
