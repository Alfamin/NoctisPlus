Free Music Finder and Lyric Motion are now part of Noctis Plus itself instead of add-ons. Search is one page for your library and online music, artist and album pages show what you don't own yet, downloads have their own page, the lyrics have a "Sync" button, and Telegram has a proper sign-in.

## Install

Paste this into PowerShell (no administrator needed):

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

It downloads `NoctisPlus-v2.0.0-Setup.exe` from this release through your system proxy/VPN, checks it against `SHA256SUMS`, installs it for your user and starts it. Or download the Setup below and run it yourself. The Setup is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time: **More info > Run anyway**.

Already on 1.6.3.1? Noctis Plus offers this update by itself. Noctis Plus now has its own version number; it is still based on Noctis 1.6.3.

Noctis Plus installs next to official Noctis (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Noctis Plus`, data in `%APPDATA%\Noctis Plus`) and never changes, updates or removes it. The first start copies your official Noctis profile (library, play counts, playlists, sign-ins); official Noctis is only read.

## What's new

- **One Search page.** Your library and online music in one place, with Songs / Artists / Albums filters. The shortcut (Ctrl+Shift+F) and a song's "Find more by this artist…" open it.
- **Artist and album pages from the online catalog.** Your own albums list the songs they're missing; online albums and artists open as normal Noctis pages with download buttons. Browsing never downloads anything.
- **Downloads page** from Search's Downloads button: progress, and finished songs play right from the list.
- **Spotify and Deezer playlist import** in a native window that matches what you already have and offers the missing songs.
- **Telegram sign-in** in Noctis's own style: phone number, code, two-step password, and clear messages when Telegram can't be reached. Settings > Features > Free Music Finder > **Telegram account** opens it any time to see who's signed in or disconnect.
- **Lyric Motion is built into the lyrics**, looking the same as the plugin, and stays in step with the music after quick pause/play. Right-to-left lines (Persian, Arabic, Hebrew) are laid out correctly in left-to-right songs, and in the mini player.
- **Lyrics "Sync" button.** Scroll the lyrics and they keep following while the current line is still on screen. Scroll it out of view and a Sync button appears, staying until you press it or scroll back; no more snapping back after a few seconds.
- **For you** shows a progress ring with the step count while it refreshes.
- **Downloads** wait as long as Music Hunters actually needs, and the download queue is saved safely when Noctis closes.

Features live under **Settings > Features**, each with its own switch; your settings from 1.6.3.1 carry over. If you installed the separate Lyric Motion or Free Music Finder plugin, the built-in one is used instead, never both.

Don't run official Noctis and Noctis Plus at the same time: both use the same Telegram session, which can sign both out, and both would scrobble to Last.fm.

## Files

- `NoctisPlus-v2.0.0-Setup.exe`: the installer.
- `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip`: portable copy; extract to a new folder and run `Noctis.exe`.
- `SHA256SUMS`: checksums for both.
