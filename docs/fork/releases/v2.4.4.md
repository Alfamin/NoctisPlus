**Noctis Plus 2.4.4**: Search that knows who you mean, QR-code Telegram sign-in and every setup on the first page.

## What's new

- **Search knows who you mean.** The top result is the song or artist people actually play, even while you are still typing: "plot twis" finds Drake's Plot Twist, "kanye" Kanye West, "beatles" The Beatles, "kiss" the band. The words decide what could be meant; how many people listen decides which.
- **Sign in to Telegram with a QR code.** Scan it in Telegram on your phone (Settings › Devices › Link Desktop Device). No app id or hash to find; your own still works under Advanced, and the phone number sign-in is one click away. Two-step passwords are asked in the same window.
- **Every setup on the first page.** A new install offers Connect Telegram and Word lyrics (1.3 GB, one press) beside Find music, Add music folder and Import playlists.
- **Word timing eases off when the PC is busy.** While a game or another app slows the models down they rest more, and wait when memory runs low (Settings › Features › WordLyrics, on by default). On the processor they now need about 3 GB instead of 8 GB.

## Fixed

- New installs download MP3 by default. Anyone who used Free Music Finder before keeps their choice.
- A graphics card or driver that keeps crashing word timing as it starts is set aside for the processor for three days, instead of failing every song.

## Validation

Application suite: zero failures. Finder: 1,685 checks, zero failures. Live Deezer searches on a fresh cache: 172 of 175 (famous artists with namesake bands and artists, half-typed names and titles) lead with what is meant. Fresh first-run profiles: the first-page card, QR sign-in window and fallback, MP3 default. Word timing measured on a 20 GB graphics card, a built-in graphics chip and the processor, and under load from a second program on the same card.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.4.4-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
