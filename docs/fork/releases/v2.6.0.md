**Noctis Plus 2.6.0**: Choose your word timing, and lyrics ready before the song starts.

## What's new

- **Choose your word timing.** The credit under the lyrics ("Word timing from Kugou") is now a small menu. Time the song with WordLyrics from there; the online timing stays on screen until WordLyrics' is ready, and both are kept, so you can switch between them either way. Songs WordLyrics timed are credited too.
- **Online timing for songs already timed.** A library song WordLyrics timed before can look for an online timing from the same menu (Find online word timing): found, it shows at once, and WordLyrics' timing stays a choice.
- **Lyrics ready for the next songs.** While a song plays, the next two in the queue are looked up online, even before they download, so their words show as soon as they start.

## Fixed

- Songs with "(feat. …)", accents or curly apostrophes in the title are found online (BIA "LONDON", Drake "What's Next", Aminé "REEL IT IN").
- Kugou picks the explicit version over the clean one.
- A song with only plain lyrics in its file is looked up online before WordLyrics times it.
- Time with WordLyrics on a streamed song waits for the download, shows its progress and starts by itself.
- No false "needs attention" before a timing starts; no "Lyrics queued" flash when the playing song goes first.
- A streamed song's length is read from the part already downloaded, so its online timing is not rejected.
- Not for me stays on a For You song played again with Previous; Reset learned preferences keeps the songs you dismissed.
- WordLyrics gets only the sung lines from an online timing (no speaker or translation marks).

## Validation

Application suite: 7,996 tests, zero failures. Finder: 1,724 checks, zero failures. Live checks on the test build: download wait and automatic start (Outside), next-song look-ahead (Nail Tech shown at once), Not for me after Previous, dismissed song kept after a reset.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.6.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
