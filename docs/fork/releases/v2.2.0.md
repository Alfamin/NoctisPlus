**Noctis Plus 2.2.0.** Every song in your playlists plays, even the ones you don't have yet.

## What's new

- **Every song in your playlists plays.** Play or Shuffle an imported playlist and the songs you don't have play too, like a streaming app.
  - **In queue order:** what you shuffled or added to the queue comes first, not the playlist's order.
  - **Ahead of time:** the next two download while you listen and the two after them wait ready with the bot, so skipping rarely waits; a download you skip past is kept half-done and goes on later.
  - **Every shuffle too:** No repeats, Discovery and the other True Shuffle modes go through the songs you don't have as well.
  - **A round button next to Shuffle** turns it off for that playlist, album or artist; then only your own songs play there.
- **Play just streams.** A song you play from Search waits in a cache (7, 30 or 90 days, or forever: Settings) instead of joining Songs. Press **Add to songs** to keep it. Adding a played song to a playlist keeps it too.
- **For you in seconds.** Recommendations fill in about 7 s instead of over a minute, and songs credited to several artists count for the right one.
- **Hard names found.** A title neither bot finds gets one more, plainer search. A close match is kept, marked **?** in Songs and listed under **Check these** in Downloads: listen, then **It's right** or **Not it**.
- **Word timing, under control.** **Restart** stops every WordLyrics run and starts again; **Time all songs without word timing** queues the rest; waiting songs say when they try again; a stuck run restarts by itself.
- **Space back.** Word timing's song copies are removed once they are exact duplicates (2.2 GB on one test PC).
- **The player fits.** In a narrow window its extra buttons step aside, and come back when there's room.
- **What's new.** After each update, a short list of what changed.

## Fixed

- Songs download about twice as fast.
- Songs whose long titles Telegram cuts short download again.
- A playlist's song that was only played joins Songs when it is added to a playlist.

## Install or update

Existing Noctis Plus installations can update through the app. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.2.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
