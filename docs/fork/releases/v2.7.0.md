**Noctis Plus 2.7.0**: based on Noctis 1.6.7, with phone sign-in and Lyrics Reels.

## New

- **Connect a phone.** Settings > Account & Devices shows a QR code; scan it with the Noctis phone app (beta) to sign in. The phone syncs with the computer, can hand the play queue back and forth, and its plays are sent on to Last.fm through the computer.
- **Lyrics Reels.** Make a Reel in any song's menu exports a lyric video with the song's audio. Layouts: Side by side, Compact header, Full page. Backgrounds: Flowing cover or Aurora.
- **From Noctis 1.6.6 and 1.6.7:** New in Library row and adjustable album rows on Home, play count import from Last.fm and ListenBrainz, marking a song as having no lyrics, configurable song columns, ReplayGain scan improvements, music video placement, the plugin submission checker and the Plugins section in the sidebar.

## Fixed

- Streamed songs keep sharp covers.
- For you pages stay until you remove them.
- Every heart is kept.
- Lyrics from Kugou and NetEase that belong to another song are rejected.

Plus behaviour is kept where the two differ: the Plus lyrics page and motion, streaming queue, Last.fm session handling and built-in features. The stock Lyric Motion and True Shuffle plugins are hidden because Plus has them built in. Exact matching of Reel words to the lyrics page is still being worked on.

## Validation

Application suite: 9,483 tests, zero failures (83 skipped). Finder: 1,760 checks, zero failures. Live TEST check: lyrics page, pause and resume, shuffle modes, Settings > Features, Plugins and About. Reels were exported and played back. Phone sign-in was not checked live before release. Release packaging and installation are checked by CI before publication.

## Install or update

Existing installations can update through Noctis Plus. For a new installation, paste into PowerShell:

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Alfamin/NoctisPlus/main/scripts/install.ps1 | iex
```

Downloads: `NoctisPlus-v2.7.0-Setup.exe`, `NoctisPlus-windows-x64.zip` and `SHA256SUMS`.
